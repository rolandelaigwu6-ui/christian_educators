Care & safeguarding

People come before profiles.

The teachers and families behind this work deserve care, dignity and control over what is shared about them.

What we publish, and what we keep private.

Permission comes first

Photos, stories and financial details are shared only with written permission from the adult involved, or a parent or guardian for a child.

Only what needs to be shared

Child profiles use a first name or agreed alias, age range and general location. Full names, exact addresses and full birth dates are not published.

Support goes through our team

Please arrange sponsorship, messages and gifts through COCEGN. We do not publish private contact details for children or families.

Something doesn’t feel right?

Contact COCEGN if you are concerned about a published story, a request for support, or the use of your information. You can ask for a story or image to be reviewed.
