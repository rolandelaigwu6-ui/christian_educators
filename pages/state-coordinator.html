Become a State Coordinator

Bring this to your state.

COCEGN started with teachers in one Local Government Area of Benue State. It was never meant to stay there. If you're a Christian educator, pastor, or advocate in another state — or another country — who reads this and thinks *"my community needs this too,"* we want to talk to you.

Apply to Become a State Coordinator

Build a community where you are.

What a State Coordinator does

  • Represents COCEGN and builds a local chapter of the network in their state
  • Connects local Christian educators to the network's programs and conferences
  • Identifies teachers, widows, and vulnerable children in their state who need support
  • Champions COCEGN's work locally — in churches, schools, and communities

Who we’re looking for

  • A practising Christian educator, minister, or passionate advocate for teachers' welfare
  • Someone already trusted and connected within their local education or church community
  • Willing to volunteer time to build something from the ground up, the way COCEGN itself began
