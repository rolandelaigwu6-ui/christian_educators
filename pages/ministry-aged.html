Ministry to the Aged

They gave their whole lives to a classroom. We won’t let them be forgotten.

A visit. A conversation. A friendship. Become a companion to an aged, retired or isolated educator.

Be someone who keeps showing up.

COCEGN’s Ministry to the Aged pairs a companion with an educator for regular visits and friendship. It is a simple way to stand with someone whose lifetime of service deserves to be remembered.

  1. 01

    Tell us where you live and why you would like to become a companion.

  2. 02

    Our team discusses the local needs and the commitment with you.

  3. 03

    Build a friendship through regular visits and encouragement.
