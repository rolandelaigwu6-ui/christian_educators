The reality behind the calling

In too many states in Nigeria, teachers go months without pay, and even when salaries come, they're rarely enough to live on without falling into debt. It doesn't get easier with age. Many teachers retire and then wait years for a pension or gratuity earned decades of classroom mornings ago — some wait until the end of their lives and never see it. We've watched retired teachers queue for hours at government screening exercises just to prove they're still owed what's theirs, and we've lost some of them to nothing more than the exhaustion of standing in that crowd, still chasing a debt the system owed them.

These are the people who taught us to read, to add, to believe we could become something. It's time someone stood with them the way they stood in front of a classroom for us, year after year, for too little in return.

A real person. A practical next step.
