Common questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Membership, sponsorship, the Academy and how to get involved.

Here to help

Who can join the network?

Christian teachers, tutors and professors are at the heart of COCEGN. Educators, ministers and advocates can contact our team to discuss their place in the work.

What are the membership tiers?

Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond. Specific fees and contribution requirements are still being confirmed. You can apply now; no payment is collected with the application.

Where does COCEGN work?

Our current active base is with teachers in the Ohimini area of Benue State, Nigeria. We are growing through State Coordinators in other communities.

Is COCEGN registered?

Yes. Core Christian Educators Global Network is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission in Nigeria, RC 1546376, since 2018.

What is COCEGN Academy?

A JSS1–JSS3 school in Ikikla Agadagba, Ohimini LGA. Every student attends on full scholarship. Support funds teachers’ salaries and running costs.

Can I give or sponsor someone online?

Online checkout is not yet available. Use our contact form or WhatsApp to discuss a child’s education, a teacher’s salary, a widow’s support or another need. Costs are confirmed before you give.

Can I see individual sponsorship profiles?

Profiles will be published when approved details and written consent are available. Contact our team to discuss current needs in the meantime.

Can I volunteer from another state?

Yes. Explore becoming a State Coordinator or contact us about volunteering and partnerships in your community.

When are the next events?

Confirmed dates, venues and registration details will appear on Events & Media. You can subscribe to updates or ask the team about upcoming gatherings.

How do I access my account?

Create an account, verify your email, then sign in through Member Login. An account is separate from your membership application; the team reviews applications and discusses next steps.

My form was saved but I did not receive an email. What should I do?

If the page says your submission was received, you do not need to submit again. You can follow up with COCEGN on WhatsApp at +234 803 287 2739.
