FAQs

Questions, answered.

What educators most often ask about membership, protection, giving, and the member portal.

Membership

Who can join?

Any Christian serving in education anywhere in the world—teachers, school leaders, support staff, and education students. Retired educators are welcome too.

How much does membership cost?

Membership is offered in tiers so cost is never the barrier. See current options and sample pricing on the Membership page.

Basic membership vs. Membership with Coverage?

Basic connects you to community, resources, and prayer ministries. With Coverage adds professional protection benefits, including job-related guidance and legal consultation for covered employment matters.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes log in to the portal or contact us and we will move you to the tier that fits your season of life and work.

Protection and help

I am facing a conflict at work. What first?

Members with Coverage should begin on the Get Help page, which routes you to job-protection guidance. Avoid signing or agreeing to anything before speaking with us.

Does membership replace a union?

We are a professional association, not a labor union community, encouragement, and protection rather than collective bargaining.

Can you give me legal advice?

Our staff are not your attorneys. Covered matters are connected with attorneys through the protection program; general questions are always welcome.

Donations

Is my gift tax-deductible?

We are organized as a 501(c)(3) religious non-profit, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Receipts come from our giving platform.

Where does my gift go?

Designate it to where most needed, scholarships and grants, global outreach, or resource development. Administrative costs stay lean.

Can I give monthly?

Yes monthly giving sustains the mission best, and you can change or cancel anytime.

Member portal

First-time login?

Use the email you joined with on the Member Login page. While the site is in development the portal runs in demonstration mode.

Forgot my password.

Choose "Forgot password?" on the login page and a reset link goes to your email.

Renew or update details?

Log in and open your dashboard—renewal, tier changes, and contact updates live there.
