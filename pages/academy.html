Keep a classroom open

The story

There was nowhere to continue. We built one.

Before COCEGN Academy, children in Ikikla Agadagba, in the Ohimini Local Government Area of Benue State, finished primary school and simply stopped. There was no secondary school in their community — no JSS1 to walk to, nowhere close enough to continue. For many families, that was the end of their child's education, not because the child couldn't learn, but because the school didn't exist.

We built one.

COCEGN Academy runs JSS1 to JSS3, right there in the community. Every single student is on scholarship — no family pays a fee to send their child here. What it costs to keep the doors open is what it costs to pay the teachers who show up every day to teach them. That's what your support actually funds: not a building, not an abstract "education program" — a teacher's salary, this month, so a child in Ikikla Agadagba can keep learning.
