Adopt a widow

She stood behind him for 30 years. Now stand behind her.

When a teacher dies, his widow is often left with no income and no plan. "Adopting" a widow means committing to support her, from wherever you are — no travel required, just a real, ongoing commitment.

A real person. A practical next step.

How it works

  1. 01

    Talk with our team about widows of late teachers who need support.

  2. 02

    Choose monthly support, a specific need or a one-time gift.

  3. 03

    We keep you connected with updates on how she is doing.
