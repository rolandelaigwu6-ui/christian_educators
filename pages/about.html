About us

Supporting Christians who serve in education.

Christian Educators exists to encourage, connect, and support educators as they live out their calling in schools.

OUR WHY

Educators matter

Educators shape lives every day and deserve a community that sees and supports them.

OUR WORK

Connection and support

We bring together people, resources, and opportunities for educators to grow.

OUR HOPE

Confident service

We want Christian educators to serve with wisdom, courage, and hope.

A place for educators to be known, equipped, and encouraged.
