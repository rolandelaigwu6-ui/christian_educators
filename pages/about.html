About us

Supporting Christians who serve in education everywhere.

Christian Educators Global Network exists to encourage, equip, connect, and protect educators as they live out their calling in schools around the world.

OUR BELIEF

Educators matter to God

We believe God has a purpose for every Christian educator and has placed them in their school on purpose.

OUR MISSION

Encourage, equip, connect, protect

We support educators through community, practical resources, prayer, and member protection.

OUR METHOD

Training, support, coverage

Strategic initiatives, training, and legal consultation coverage for members in covered tiers.

OUR VISION

Christ in every school

A global network of educators serving students, families, and colleagues with wisdom, courage, and hope.

What we do

Four commitments to every member.

01

Connect

Community with educators who understand your faith and your work—in your nation and across the world.

02

Equip

Devotionals, rights guidance, classroom encouragement, training, and a growing resource library.

03

Protect

Job-related guidance and professional consultation coverage for members in covered tiers.

04

Advocate

A respectful voice for the freedom of Christians to live their faith in education.

Our story

From a living room to a global network.

  • The beginning

    It started with educators around a table

    Christian educators began meeting to pray for their schools and to encourage one another, convinced that no educator should stand alone.

  • Growth

    Community became a network

    Gatherings multiplied, publications began, and the vision of supporting Christians in education took organizational shape.

  • Protection

    Protection for the moments that matter

    Membership grew to include job-related guidance and professional-consultation coverage, giving educators confidence to serve faithfully.

  • Going global

    One network, many nations

    The movement expanded beyond one country resources, prayer movements, and gatherings now serve educators worldwide.

  • Today

    Equipping the next generation

    We continue to encourage, equip, connect, and protect Christians in education students, teachers, leaders, and retirees alike.
