The story behind COCEGN

I've been a teacher long enough to know something most people outside the classroom don't: teaching in a Christian school or community isn't just a career. It's a calling, and it comes with a cost most people never see.

I started COCEGN in 2018 because I kept watching that cost land hardest on the people least able to carry it — a widow left with nothing after her husband, a teacher, passed away. Children who lost a parent and lost their school fees in the same year. Retired teachers who spent decades shaping other people's children and were then quietly forgotten.

We began small, with teachers here in Benue State. That's still where we're strongest. But the name we chose — Core Christian Educators Global Network — was never a name we picked to sound bigger than we were. It was a direction. A network as wide as the calling itself, wherever there's a Christian teacher trying to be faithful in a classroom.

We're a registered organisation (CAC RC 1546376, since 2018), and everything we do — the conferences, the sponsorships, the visits to aged teachers nobody else checks on — comes from one conviction: God's Word doesn't stop at the classroom door, and neither should the way we care for each other.

Amb. Dr. Mark Alechenu Elaigwu, Founder & Initiator

Meet our founder

MEFounder & initiator

Amb. Dr. Mark Alechenu Elaigwu

Amb. Dr. Mark Alechenu Elaigwu is a native of Benue State, Nigeria, and a Minister of the Gospel who has spent 18 years in the classroom, in school administration, and in consulting and facilitation for the education sector. He holds a Ph.D. in Biology Education from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

His work has been recognised with a State Award of Excellence, a National Award, the Millennium Award, the Development Goals Award, an Honorary Doctorate in Education, the African International Achievers Award, and Ambassadorial honours conferred in the UK in 2011. He is the Founder and Initiator of Core Christian Educators Global Network.

What guides us

Our mission

To inspire and support Christian educators and encourage community development by providing opportunities for spiritual and professional growth based on God's Word.

Our vision

Equipped and encouraged educators according to Biblical principles, transforming our schools with God's love and Truth.

One community today. Every state tomorrow.

Our active work is with teachers connected to the Ohimini area of Benue State. COCEGN Academy is in Ikikla Agadagba, Ohimini LGA. Our registration is in Otukpo, Benue State; we do not currently operate a physical office there.

We’re growing through State Coordinators who bring this work to their own communities, one state at a time.
