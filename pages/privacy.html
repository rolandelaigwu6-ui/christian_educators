Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create a member account, join, donate, or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, organization or school, and the content of your messages. Like most websites, we also receive basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate region.

How we use information

Provide the mission

To deliver resources, events, prayer ministries, and member services you request.

Process support

To process memberships and donations through trusted, secure payment providers.

Communicate

To send updates you have asked for. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our membership and payment platforms) and only as needed to provide the services you requested. We use industry standard safeguards, and payment card details are handled exclusively by our payment providers they are never stored on this website.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time, and you may opt out of email communications using the link in any message. To make a request, contact us through the Get Help page. We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be announced on this page.
