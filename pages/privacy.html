Privacy information

Your information deserves care.

This page explains the information collected by the current website and how to contact COCEGN about it.

What you share with us

Membership applications collect your name, email, membership interest, role and location, plus optional phone, school or organisation, and church information. We also record your application and values agreements, and whether you ask for news and updates.

Contact, coordinator, volunteer, partnership and story forms save the details you enter so our team can review and respond. Newsletter subscriptions save your email and, if provided, first name.

Account registration collects your name, email and password. Passwords are stored as protected hashes. Signing in sets a session cookie needed to keep you signed in.

How the website uses it

Applications and enquiries are available to authorised administrators. Confirmation emails may be sent to you, and an internal notification tells the team that a submission is waiting. Notification emails do not include your enquiry or story text.

Submitting a story does not publish it. Our team will contact you before public use. The care and safeguarding page describes the approach to sharing stories and images of teachers and families.

This site does not currently include advertising or analytics scripts. Following a WhatsApp link takes you to WhatsApp, which operates its own service and privacy terms.

Questions, corrections or stopping updates?

Use our contact form or call +234 803 287 2739 if you need to correct your details, discuss their use, request deletion, or stop receiving updates.
