A school. A next chapter.

A secondary school where one never existed.

In Ikikla Agadagba, children used to finish primary school with no secondary school in their community to move on to. COCEGN Academy was built to change that.

Every student attends on full scholarship. Your support pays the teachers and running costs that keep their classrooms open.
