Who we are

Faith, education and care.
A shared commitment.

Core Christian Educators Global Network inspires and supports Christian educators through opportunities for spiritual and professional growth based on God’s Word.

Rooted in Benue State, our work connects educator development with practical care for teachers, children, widows and aged members of the community.

COCEGN Academy

Opening a path to secondary education.

In Ikikla Agadagba, children used to finish primary school with no secondary school in their community to move on to. COCEGN Academy was built to change that.

Every student attends on full scholarship. Your support pays the teachers and running costs that keep their classrooms open.
